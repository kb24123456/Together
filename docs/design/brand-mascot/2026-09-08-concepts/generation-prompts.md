## Quiet Pebble

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original animated brand mascot concept sheet for a restrained native iPhone task app.
Create one polished character design direction internally named "Quiet Pebble". This is character art exploration, not app UI, not an implementation diagram.
Input image 1 is only inspiration for a clean black round body and white eyes. Input image 2 is only inspiration for softness and emotional warmth. Develop an original identity; do not duplicate either reference character.
Target: a square 1536 x 1536 art-directed presentation, white background, generous whitespace, sharp clean silhouettes. One single character design shown consistently in different poses.
LAYOUT: a large lovely neutral hero portrait centered in the upper half, about 420px across. Underneath, a balanced row of three smaller poses of this EXACT SAME character: eyes softly closed in a blink; actively writing with a tiny hand holding one clearly visible pencil; a subtly worried expression with one sweat droplet. At the bottom, two tiny scale-study groups, one on white and one on a small flat dark charcoal swatch, repeating the neutral and writing pose at roughly 40px body size. Do not make a phone mockup. No grids, cards, panel borders, text, logos, numbered labels or watermarks.
CHARACTER: a compact black almost-sphere with a very slightly organic rounded-pebble outline, a barely flattened base, width close to height, stable center of gravity. Matte almost pure black flat fill, subtle curvature from shape alone. Two small, warm, upright off-white oval eyes in the upper middle of the face, slightly asymmetric in height for gentle personality, sufficient spacing. No mouth, no nose, no ears, no antenna, no hair, no permanent limbs, no humanoid second torso. It should feel quietly attentive, understated, lovable and iconic rather than a generic robot or a sinister black void. The expression and silhouette should be beautiful even before animation.
POSES: retain identical proportions and eyes across poses. Hands appear only for writing, as minimal soft rounded off-white mitten shapes, short black arms attached naturally to the sphere. Hold a simple cream/light-gray pencil with a dark tip at a diagonal across the lower-right side; the grip must be credible and obvious; a tiny curved writing stroke can imply action, no desk or notebook obscuring the body. For concern use a slight head lean, expressive white eyes and one pale-gray teardrop sweat accent, not fear or crying. On dark background maintain black identity with a restrained charcoal rim.
Style: premium minimal 2D editorial character design, vector-friendly geometry, smooth precise edges, monochrome. Avoid fuzzy fur, glossy 3D plastic, realistic lighting, heavy gradients, glow, blur, drop shadows, shiny specular spots that could be confused with eyes, rainbow accents, busy props. Prioritize small-scale beauty and simple animatable parts. Each pose is the same single design, not different concepts.

## Soft Dumpling

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original animated brand mascot concept sheet for a restrained native iPhone task app.
Create one polished character design direction internally named "Soft Dumpling". Character design, not app UI, not a technical diagram.
Input image 1 inspires only the black orb and minimalist white eyes; input image 2 inspires the soft rounded silhouette and innocent quiet personality. Reinterpret these cues into an original black character rather than copying a reference.
Target: square 1536 x 1536 canvas, pure white background, very generous negative space, clean polished visual presentation. Show ONE consistent character identity with several expressive poses.
LAYOUT: one large neutral hero centered in the upper half, body roughly 440px wide. Underneath, a spacious horizontal row of three smaller poses of this SAME character: a gentle eyes-closed blink with body slightly compressed; diligently writing with a tiny hand and one pencil; mildly concerned with one sweat droplet. At the bottom, two small scale-study groups, one white and one on a small simple dark charcoal swatch, each showing the same neutral and writing poses at approximately 40px body size. No phone shell, no UI, no headings, words, numeric labels, logos, panel borders or watermarks.
CHARACTER: an irresistibly gentle black soft ball, slightly wider than tall (roughly 1.13:1), broad softly domed top, round full cheeks integrated into the main sphere, a subtly compressed bottom edge like soft mochi resting gently. ONE continuous rounded body only, no head-and-body snowman, no feet, no ears, no hair tuft, no tail. Two small warm-white circular/short oval eyes with generous spacing, low enough on the upper-middle face to feel innocently attentive, curious and approachable. Slight natural asymmetry keeps it from looking like an emoji. No mouth, no nose, no blush. Almost pure black matte body with a very restrained charcoal curved tonal edge only if useful. The main design should read as a lovable living black orb with softness communicated through the outline, not fuzz or glowing blur.
Writing pose: the same character leans a little forward and squashes gently, two tiny creamy-white soft mitten hands emerge on short black arms, one naturally gripping a single pale-gray pencil with dark tip, the other gently balances. Keep the arms and hands extremely simple and large enough to read at icon size. A short curved graphite stroke in front can suggest writing, no large sheet of paper or furniture. Concern pose: a slightly tilted/compressed body, raised gentle white eyes and a single pale sweat droplet, looking earnest and a little sheepish, never scared, accusing, panicked or sad.
Style: beautifully proportioned premium minimalist 2D vector-friendly mascot, crisp edges, flat monochrome, calm native-app aesthetic. It must look delightfully soft but not childish or candy-like. Preserve the face and silhouette across all poses. Dark swatch version stays black with a subtle lighter perimeter. Avoid furry soot sprite, famous character copies, strong gradients, plastic reflections, 3D rendering, strong shadows, glowing eye effects, mouths, yellow pencil, colored cheeks, extra decorative items.

## Curious Inkdrop

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original animated brand mascot concept sheet for a minimalist native iPhone to-do app.
Create one sophisticated character design direction internally named "Curious Inkdrop". This is a single original mascot identity with several poses, not an app screen or a family of different mascots.
The attached reference image 1 supplies only the clean black spherical mass and white eye simplicity; reference image 2 supplies only fluid softness. Create a distinct original silhouette and personality from those broad cues, do not reproduce either reference mascot.
Target dimensions: square 1536 x 1536. White background, premium art-directed whitespace. One large neutral hero centered in the upper half, about 420px in diameter. Below, three neatly spaced smaller poses of this EXACT SAME character: a gentle blink with closed eyes; writing industriously with a tiny hand and pencil; mild concern with one sweat droplet. At the bottom two tiny scale-study groups, one on white, the other on a small flat dark charcoal swatch, showing neutral and writing at approximately 40px body size. No device or interface, no captions, typography, numbers, logo, watermark, panel borders or extra ornament.
CHARACTER: a black living ink orb, 95% round, soft full balloon-like lower body, with ONE tiny integrated off-center upward curl/taper in the upper-right perimeter as if a droplet is gently pulled by surface tension. The taper must be small, rounded and integral, not hair, a sharp horn, an ear, a flame or a teardrop replacing the whole sphere. A quiet asymmetrical silhouette gives a recognizable brand signature. Slight forward tilt and a subtle off-center face give a bright curious attentive personality. Two off-white small rounded vertical capsule eyes, slightly shorter and playfully offset, placed together toward the right half without touching the edge. No mouth, nose, eyebrows, ears, legs, torso or permanent hands. Matte almost-black fill, clean slightly calligraphic vector silhouette, no fuzzy surface.
For writing: the sphere leans forward attentively with the same tiny top curl and same eye shapes, two simple rounded off-white mitten hands emerge naturally on short black arms, one properly gripping a clearly readable pale cream pencil with dark graphite tip, pencil pointed down to a small graphite curved stroke. Convey energetic concentration through pose, not motion blur. No desk, no notebook, no props other than the pencil. For concern: one small sweat droplet near the upper-left outer contour, eyes slightly narrowed/widened expressively but still friendly and earnest, not menacing or distressed. Blink pose retains body identity.
Style: extremely polished minimal 2D brand character exploration, sharp vector-friendly edges and playful restrained shape design, mostly flat black/off-white, enough negative space to inspect the character. Clear at tiny icon size. On the dark swatch use a subtle charcoal edge and keep the body black. Avoid copying Grok, ghost bodies, soot sprites, pets, complex anatomy, a giant tail, flamelike silhouette, 3D gloss, material textures, gradients, glow, ambient blur, shadows, colorful props, text.
