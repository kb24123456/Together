# 第二轮：圆形球体与球面眼睛

日期：2026-09-08。工具：内置 image_gen；未使用 CLI fallback，未自行指定或验证具体模型版本。

## 用户明确反馈

- 第一轮三个方向全部不满意，均不作为后续实施目标。
- 眼睛太小；希望更大的白色长眼睛，并且贴合在球体曲面上。
- 要圆圆滚滚、具有立体球体感觉的黑球；不要平面图形、扁底或不够圆的轮廓。
- 不要第三版顶部的小角或其他突起。
- 本轮用户提供的图 1 和图 2 是最优主参考；后续彩色团子仅参考柔软、亲近的气质。

## 本轮范围

先生成静态主体，集中验证球形、眼睛大小、曲面透视和克制体积感。手、笔、汗滴与动画暂缓，避免尚未满意的主体被其他内容分散注意力。本轮任何候选仍须用户评价，不自动视为定稿。

## 完整提示词

### Spherical Quiet

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: refined static hero portrait of a black spherical app mascot.
This is a corrective second design round. The user's main references are attached images 1 and 2; their shape and spherical eyes are the target, not merely a loose mood. Attached images 3 and 4 are SECONDARY references for gentle soft friendly personality ONLY. Do not use the pastel colors, flattened dumpling bases, blushing cheeks, toys, rooms, or illustrative backgrounds of images 3 and 4.
The previous concepts failed because they had tiny eyes, flattened/squarish bottoms and a horn. Correct those failures decisively.
COMPOSITION: square 1024 x 1024 clean white background. ONE single complete black ball centered in the frame, occupying around 62% of the canvas width. All of the circular silhouette must be visible with generous whitespace. No contact with a floor, no cropped bottom, no pedestal, no floor shadow. No contact pressure to flatten the ball. No scene, diagram, captions, labels, typography, watermark, grids or extra poses.
BODY: a true smooth round SPHERE with a circular 1:1 silhouette, like a perfectly round soft black ball floating quietly. The lower arc must be just as round and continuous as the upper arc. Absolutely no flat base, rounded-square outline, jelly dome, bean, pear, ghost tail, tip, horn, hair, tuft, ears or protrusions. Deep neutral black material, velvety smooth in spirit but no fibers, fur, seams or surface texture. Softness from roundness and expression.
EYES: exactly TWO large solid warm-white elongated round-ended capsule/ribbon shapes on the upper hemisphere, with no pupils, iris, eyelids, outlines or sockets. Eye length about 21–25% of ball diameter and width about 8–10% of diameter, clearly much larger than small dots. They are white markings lying FLUSH along the curved skin of the sphere, not separate floating eyes or raised eyeballs. Their curved outlines, tilt, apparent length and width must be a consistent PERSPECTIVE PROJECTION on the SAME convex spherical surface: the nearer eye larger and wider, farther eye narrower and a little shorter, gently converging meridian orientation, slight height offset. They should visibly wrap around the ball, allowing us to infer its orientation from the eyes alone. Do not draw two identical parallel frontal pills pasted onto a flat disk.
ART DIRECTION: match the economy and appeal of attached black-ball references. This is a black spherical character with subtle real volume and a quiet soft personality. Do not let '3D' turn it into a plastic product render, glossy bowling ball, camera robot or reflective metal ornament. No extra white lighting reflections that might read as additional eyes. No glass, chrome, bright rim lights, giant specular softbox highlights, strong gradients, harsh shadows or glow. A barely perceptible charcoal tonal rolloff may support the spherical volume, but the primary depth cue must be the surface-conforming eyes. No mouth, nose, arms, hands, pencil, sweat, legs, accessories, blush or colored accents in this round. Focus all attention on the perfect round ball and the beautiful larger eyes.

DIRECTION: "Spherical Quiet". Stay particularly close to attached primary reference 1 in pose and visual simplicity. The face looks gently upward and toward the viewer's right, placed in the upper-right hemisphere, with comfortable space from the edge. The near eye is on the left of the eye pair and has a long subtly curved capsule shape; the far eye to its right is narrower and foreshortened around the globe. Both lean naturally down toward the right as they follow the spherical meridians. A small difference in their top alignment communicates a rounded rotating globe. Keep a nearly solid black body with only extremely restrained shading. The expression should be quietly attentive and charming, never anxious.
